- **red skill: interactive fix-review flow** — open suggestion (Nov 13 2026) from a comrade session where a go-ahead got spent on a *description* of the fix and the whole refactor arrived as one preview wall. The skill's "propose the fix in one line" never required the proposal to be the literal edit — a summary satisfies it, and a summary is where trust leaks. Proposed flow: (1) summarize/group the check's findings into fix-shaped clusters, each with an approach; (2) ask if that's the goal, with options for how to approach; (3) review in small steps — one chunk at a time, before/after chunks only, no walls of changes, one file/concern per step, a go per chunk, apply, next; (4) re-run the check when the group is done, not per chunk. Open tension worth settling while drafting: weight — full diffs are heavy for a two-character autocorrectable; lean is mechanics get a one-liner, anything touching behavior or structure gets shown as the literal edit.
